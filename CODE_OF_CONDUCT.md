# Code of Conduct

We welcome participants of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
patient and constructive. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
Respect others' privacy and accept reasonable feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. This policy
applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at **krishnansidhaarth@gmail.com**.
Reports are handled confidentially as far as practical. The maintainer may remove
content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or ban participants
based on the severity and repeated nature of the conduct. Maintainers follow the
same standards and consider context before taking action.
